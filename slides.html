Big if True Science (BiTS) Accelerator

Supporting ARPA-like
managed research programs

BiTS supports the development and launch of more ARPA-like managed research programs. The program recruits early to mid-career research talent with big-if-true ideas, helps them develop into program leaders with actionable research visions, and matches them to a host institution like DARPA, ARPA-H, ARIA, ARPA-E, and FROs.

Curriculum
We use a curriculum developed by experienced ARPA PMs and historians and a practical course on program design.
Mentorship
We connect participants to experienced former ARPA PMs and provide networking opportunities.
Events
Fellows participate in various events including a Demo Day to conclude the experience.

About the program

BiTS is running a dedicated cohort in partnership with SPRIND — Germany's Federal Agency for Disruptive Innovation. Fellows will develop actionable research visions and be matched to host institutions including DARPA, ARPA-H, ARIA, ARPA-E, and FROs.
